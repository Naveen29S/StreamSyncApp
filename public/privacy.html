Privacy Policy for StreamSync

Effective Date: September 5, 2026[cite: 1]
Developer: Naveen[cite: 1]

This Privacy Policy explains how StreamSync collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our cross-platform (iOS, Android, Web) social media analytics and management application[cite: 1].

1. Information We Collect

To provide a unified dashboard for content creators, we collect and process the following data:

  • Account Information: Your email address and full display name when you sign up via Supabase Auth or Google OAuth[cite: 1].
  • Authentication Data: API keys, OAuth tokens (such as Google provider tokens), and channel IDs required to sync your connected platforms[cite: 1].
  • Platform Analytics: Aggregated metrics including lifetime total views, subscriber/follower counts, engagement rates, and estimated revenue[cite: 1].
  • Content & Comments: Post titles, thumbnail URLs, publish dates, view counts, and the text, author names, and avatars of top comments from recent videos[cite: 1].

2. How We Use Your Data

StreamSync strictly uses this information to deliver its core features:

  • Data Aggregation: To track performance, surface top comments, and calculate engagement rates and revenue estimates[cite: 1].
  • Real-Time Syncing: To keep your dashboard updated using Supabase Realtime subscriptions and background Edge Functions[cite: 1].
  • Post Management: To allow you to schedule and track future posts across connected platforms[cite: 1].

3. Data Security & Third-Party Services

Your privacy and data isolation are critical to our architecture:

  • Secure Infrastructure: All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase, protected by Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure strict per-user data isolation[cite: 1].
  • API Integrations: We use the YouTube Data API v3 to fetch your channel data[cite: 1]. OAuth tokens are never logged, and the system prevents stale token reuse[cite: 1].
  • Data Deletion: If you choose to remove your account, our cascade delete system ensures all of your user data is permanently erased[cite: 1].
